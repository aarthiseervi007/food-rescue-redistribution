ABOUT THE PROJECT

Technology with a purpose.

Food Rescue and Redistribution System is a student project that demonstrates how digital listings can help communities identify surplus food and coordinate potential redistribution.

Primary goal: SDG 2 — Zero Hunger

Supporting goals: SDG 12 — Responsible Consumption and Production; SDG 17 — Partnerships for the Goals

Project presenter: Aarthi O
